Fantastic Chronicle — Art Direction Samples v0.1

Every image below is drawn at SNES resolution from indexed pixel data, then scaled by a whole number with no smoothing. Nothing is a photograph of the real game; all art is original.

1. Hero sprite palette (15 colors + transparent)

One 4bpp palette per sprite, as on the SNES. Colors are kept to the SNES 5-bit-per-channel grid (multiples of 8). A palette swap of the same sprite produces a second hero, which is how the eight protagonists and their successors can share animation frames.

2. Card samples, one per card type

Cards are 112×144 pixels. Blue gradient window with a white, grey and dark bevel. A colored strip under the name marks the card type. Art window is 96×48. Below it: weapon or school icon, tier pips, cost. Combo links are shown as open and filled shapes so they stay readable without color. Text is an 8×8 bitmap font, 12 characters per line.

3. Battle screen mockup, 256×224

Side view. Enemies on the left with intent icons above them. Heroes on the right. The status strip and hand window are standard blue windows. The selected card rises four pixels and the cursor points at it. The glimmer bubble over the lead hero marks a spark this round.

4. Sprite sheet

Hero battle sprite at 32×48, about three head-heights, head roughly one third of the height. Shown at 1×, 3× and 6×, then as a palette swap. Enemy sprite is 32×40. Icons are 16×16. Glimmer bubble is 8×11.
